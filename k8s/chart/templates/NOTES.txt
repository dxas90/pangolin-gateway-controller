To verify that the controller is running:

  kubectl get pods -n {{ .Values.namespace }} -l app.kubernetes.io/name={{ include "base.name" . }}

To view controller logs:

  kubectl logs -n {{ .Values.namespace }} -l app.kubernetes.io/name={{ include "base.name" . }} -f

To check controller health:

  kubectl port-forward -n {{ .Values.namespace }} svc/{{ include "base.fullname" . }}-metrics {{ .Values.service.healthPort }}:{{ .Values.service.healthPort }}
  curl http://localhost:{{ .Values.service.healthPort }}/healthz
